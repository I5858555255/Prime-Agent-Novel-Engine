# Code of Conduct

Prime Agent contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces professional, technical, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct when discussing code, design, and decisions.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good faith while asking for evidence and clear reasoning.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials, private prompts, session data, or identifying information.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact methods listed on the [Prime Intellect website](https://www.primeintellect.ai/). Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
